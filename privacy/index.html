Privacy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Short version: no ads and no tracking. You can use the whole map without an account. If you make one, I keep only what's needed to run it.

If you create an account

  • Signing in uses your email address. I use it to sign you in and, rarely, to contact you about your account or a photo you shared. It is never shown publicly or sold.
  • Your username, display name, neighborhood and bio are public on the photos you share.
  • Photos you add, with their title, year, story and map location, are public once approved. Don't add photos that show private details you don't want shared.
  • Accounts, photos and form messages are stored with Supabase, which runs the database and storage for the map.
  • You can delete your photos any time from Your account, and delete your whole account there too. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, photos, comments and saved shots.

What stays on your device

Saved shots, likes, recently viewed pins, your addresses and appearance settings are stored in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and I can't see them. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Your location

If you tap Shots near me or Add my current location, your browser asks for permission and the map uses your location to move the view. It is not sent to me or stored anywhere else.

Other services the map loads from

To show the map and photos, your browser connects directly to a few other services. Like any website, they can see your IP address and basic browser details when they send you content:

  • Map tiles from OpenFreeMap, and satellite imagery from Esri when you switch to that view.
  • Photos from the site or archive that hosts each one, such as the New York Public Library or the Library of Congress.
  • A few archive photos pass through the image service images.weserv.nl so they display correctly.
  • Hosting by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, which keep standard server logs.
  • Supabase, which stores form messages.

Fonts and map code are hosted with the site itself, not loaded from third parties.

When you contact me

Messages from the feedback and removal forms are stored in a private database (Supabase) that only I can read. If you include your email, I use it only to reply and handle your request.

Changes

If new features change what's collected, this page will be updated before they go live.

About this map

I'm Pete Samuel, a filmmaker born and bred in Brooklyn. I love New York history and old photos of the city, but I could never find one map that tied it all together. So I built one.

Every pin is a real place. Some are corners where a movie was shot. Others are where a photographer stood fifty or a hundred years ago. Open a pin, look at the photo, then look up. That's the whole idea.

Where the photos come from

Nothing here is mine. The photos and research belong to libraries, archives, historians and bloggers who have spent years documenting this city. Every photo is credited and links straight back to where it lives, so you can read the full story on their site. You can see the full list of sources and how many photos each one has in the map menu under About and sources.

If you run one of these sites or collections and want your images handled differently, or removed, let me know here and I'll take care of it.

How accurate are the pins?

Most pins come from a street address or a named corner in the original caption. When a caption only names two cross streets, the pin goes on that corner. Some will still be off. If you spot one, tell me and I'll move it.

  • Rights holders can ask for changes or removal at any time on the request removal page.

Use it sensibly

  • Pin locations, dates and titles come from the original captions and are sometimes wrong. Don't rely on them for anything important.
  • When you go looking for a spot in person, watch the street, not your phone. Respect private property and the people who live there.

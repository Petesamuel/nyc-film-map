What this is

NYC Then and Now is a free, noncommercial map that points to historic photos and film locations published by other sites and archives. It's a personal project, run by Pete Samuel. You can browse without an account. An account lets you share your own photos.

The photos belong to their owners

  • Photos, captions and research shown on the map belong to the libraries, archives, sites and people credited on each one.
  • The map links to them where they live. Images load from the original site, and every photo links back to its source.
  • Showing an image here does not give you any rights to it. If you want to reuse a photo, check the rights statement on the source site and ask the owner.
  • Rights holders can ask for changes or removal at any time on the request removal page.

Your account and the photos you share

  • Only share photos you took, that come from your own family collection, that you have permission to share, or that are in the public domain.
  • You keep the rights to your photos. By sharing one, you let NYC Then and Now show it on the map and in the app, credited to your username, for as long as it stays up.
  • No photos that are hateful, sexual, violent, harassing, or that expose someone's private information. No spam.
  • Every photo is reviewed before it appears. I can decline or remove any photo, and suspend accounts that break these rules.
  • Anyone can report a photo. If you think something you own was shared without permission, use the request removal page.

Use it sensibly

  • Pin locations, dates and titles come from the original captions and are sometimes wrong. Don't rely on them for anything important.
  • When you go looking for a spot in person, watch the street, not your phone. Respect private property and the people who live there.
  • Please don't scrape the map, overload it, or try to break it.
